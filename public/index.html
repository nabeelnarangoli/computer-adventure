      The 5D Career Discovery Framework

      Our method, from first conversation to a plan you can act on.

      1. D1

        Discover

        What genuinely engages the student: interests, curiosities, the subjects that light them up.

      2. D2

        Diagnose

        Aptitude and personality assessment with validated instruments, interpreted by trained professionals.

      3. D3

        Define

        The career clusters that fit the profile, explained in a way students and parents both understand.

      4. D4

        Design

        The education pathway: subjects, stream, degree, university and country.

      5. D5

        Develop

        Skills, a personal action plan and ongoing mentoring, so the plan becomes real.
